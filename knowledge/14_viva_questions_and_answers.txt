# Viva Questions and Answers

Why did you choose this project? Answer with a real problem, who benefits, and what you learned.

What is RAG and why did you use it? RAG searches your own documents and gives the relevant text to the language model, so answers are grounded in your data and it works well even with a small local model.

Why a local model and not an API? Privacy, no internet needed after download, no cost, and full control for teaching.

Why SQLite? It needs no server, stores everything in one file, and is enough for a single-machine demo. It can be replaced with PostgreSQL later.

How is your password stored? As a salted hash generated by werkzeug, never as plain text.

What is an embedding? A list of numbers that represents the meaning of a text; similar meanings give nearby vectors.

What is cosine similarity? A measure of the angle between two vectors; closer to 1 means more similar.

What are the limitations? A 0.5B model can make mistakes, retrieval quality depends on the knowledge files, and CPU inference is slower than GPU.

What is future scope? Bigger model, streaming answers, voice input, feedback buttons, and cloud deployment.

How did you test it? Unit and integration tests with pytest plus a manual test table.
